📖 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new account?
Open Create New Account, add your name, phone number, email address and a password of at least 6 characters. Add a profile photo if you want, then select Save & Login.
How do I log in?
Enter your registered email or phone number, enter your password, and press Login. Your account must already be registered.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Select Forgot Password? on the login screen and enter your registered email or phone number. If an email is available for the account, a password-reset link can be sent to that email.
How can I change my password?
Open your account/settings area and use the password-change option. Keep your new password private and use a strong password that you do not reuse elsewhere.
How do I change my profile photo?
Open your profile, choose the profile-photo option, select an image from your device, and save the change.
How do I send a message?
Open Chat, choose the person you want to contact, type your message and use the send button. If the person cannot be found, check the account identifier and connection.
How do I share photos, videos or files?
Open the relevant chat and use the media/file attachment option. Choose the item from your device and confirm before sending.
How do I manage privacy?
Open Privacy & Security in settings. Review visibility options such as phone/email visibility, online status, last seen and read receipts, then choose the settings you want.
How do I block or unblock someone?
Use the user's profile/chat options to block them. Blocked users can be reviewed from the blocked-users area and unblocked when needed.
How do I delete my account?
Use the account deletion option in settings. Account deletion can be permanent, so read the confirmation carefully before continuing.
Why am I not able to log in?
Check your email/phone and password, make sure there are no extra spaces, and check your internet connection. If you forgot the password, use Forgot Password?.
How can I keep my account safe?
Use a strong private password, never share passwords or OTPs, avoid suspicious links, keep your device protected, and review privacy settings regularly.

3.

5.
